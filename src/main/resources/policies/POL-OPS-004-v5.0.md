# Payment Case Prioritisation Guide

## 4.1 Queue priority
Cases with multiple independent high-severity indicators may be prioritised for investigator review. No customer-impacting action is automatic.

## 4.2 Service targets
Training service targets are four hours for high-risk fraud investigation, twenty-four hours for payment operations and manual review, and seventy-two hours for merchant dispute operations. Targets are indicative and do not authorise any financial action.
