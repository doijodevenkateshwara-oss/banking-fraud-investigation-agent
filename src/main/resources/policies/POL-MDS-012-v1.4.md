# Merchant Dispute Operations Procedure

## 5.1 Merchant dispute eligibility
When the customer recognises the merchant but disputes fulfilment, cancellation, a refund that was not received or an incorrect amount, classify the case as a merchant dispute and route it to dispute operations rather than fraud investigation.

## 5.2 Evidence for merchant disputes
Request proof of cancellation, delivery tracking or the agreed amount from the customer, and record the merchant category and risk profile. The assistant may recommend an evidence request but must not contact the customer or the merchant.
